## Project

**P16-KLHL35 AI Annotation Pipeline**

This repository contains software developed during a research internship focused on the automated segmentation and annotation of tissue compartments in histological images using deep learning.

The project aims to assist the annotation of tissue compartments and facilitate their quantitative analysis.
